What Canada's own wage file says technical roles pay

Employment and Social Development Canada publishes a wage file covering every occupation in the National Occupational Classification. This page lifts 16 software, data, IT and computer-engineering occupations out of that file and sets the national and provincial numbers side by side. Nothing here is estimated: every figure is the file's own, printed with the period it rests on and the date this page read it.

03

What this file is, and what it is not

  • Source. The single file behind every number on this page is Employment and Social Development Canada's open-data Wages file, 2025 edition (the dataset; the CSV), released under the Open Government Licence – Canada. The file read here is 2a71-das-wage2025opendata-esdc-all-19nov2025-vf.csv, published 2025-11-19; sha256 7bd29fb41b2a84eb61a4dbf49bfb63651a8a694c62ce42ac69cb785679d66328; read 9 October 2026.
  • What the numbers are. They are wages paid, as the file itself says, not the wages employers offer in an ad. The median is the middle of the distribution, not an average of salaries.
  • The reference period is the file's, not this page's. Most cells rest on the Labour Force Survey for 2023–2024; some rest on 2024 small-area estimates or on Employment Insurance survey data. The period travels with the figure and is marked in the tables above.
  • No conversion. These occupations are published per hour, and they are kept per hour here. A year does not have a fixed number of paid hours, so turning an hourly figure into an annual one would mean inventing the multiplier.
  • What it is not. Not a job posting, not a recruiter, and not advice. It describes an occupation across a province, so a given offer, seniority level or city can sit well above or below it. It is not this site's own sample, and it is not drawn from the paid pack.

The file carries more than these 16 occupations and more than one geography — national, provincial and economic-region rows for every NOC code. It is published as a CSV and is free to reuse with attribution under the OGL–Canada; the link above is the whole source.
