02

What the counts are

  • Source. 141 Canadian technical job postings collected on 5 October 2026 — data analyst, business intelligence, data engineering, machine learning, clinical data and software roles. 140 were readable in full; 43 published the employer's own screening questions; 39 published the answer set; 53 were scored against a written fit rubric.
  • What a count is. A keyword match against those posting texts. Excel 56 means 56 of the 140 readable postings contain the word Excel. It is not a ranking of importance and not a target to hit.
  • The sample is not random. It is Ontario-heavy — Toronto, Mississauga, Markham, Oakville, Burlington — and skews toward analytics, business intelligence, clinical data and operations analysis. It is a record of what one applicant applied to, not a survey of Canadian employers.
  • What it is not. Not a keyword-stuffing guide, not a recruiter, and not a promise of an interview or a job. No employer endorsed this page or supplied anything in it.
  • Freshness. Postings expire and wordings change. The date above is the date of the data.
